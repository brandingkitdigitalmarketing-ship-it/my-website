FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

What digital marketing services does Brandingkit provide?
Brandingkit provides an end-to-end digital growth suite comprising Business Automation (CRM, AI, WhatsApp), Paid Advertising (Meta Ads, Google Ads), Search Engine Optimization (Technical, Local, AEO/GEO), Website & Landing Page Development, Social Media Marketing, and Creative Ad Production.
Can Brandingkit manage all our marketing activities?
Yes. We operate as an integrated digital growth department for startups, SMEs, and scaling enterprises, managing campaign strategy, creative production, ad buying, CRM integration, follow-up automations, and reporting under one roof.
How does your lead generation process work?
We combine precision audience targeting on Meta and Google with conversion-optimized landing pages. When a user submits an inquiry, our automated system triggers an instant WhatsApp message, syncs the lead to your CRM, and alerts your sales rep within seconds.
What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?
Google Ads delivers immediate commercial intent traffic through paid search auctions, providing instantaneous visibility. SEO builds long-term organic authority and recurring traffic without per-click fees. We often combine both for maximum search dominance.
Can you integrate CRM with WhatsApp Business?
Yes, we deploy official WhatsApp Business Platform integrations that automatically deliver brochure downloads, appointment confirmations, and AI chatbot responses while synchronizing conversation histories into your CRM in real time.
Do you develop websites for small businesses?
Yes. We create modern, fast, mobile-responsive business websites and landing pages tailored specifically to help small businesses and local clinics build trust and convert visitors into inquiries.
Can I choose individual services or a complete package?
You can engage Brandingkit for individual standalone services (such as SEO or Meta Ads management) or implement our comprehensive All-in-One Digital Growth Stack for connected marketing and automation.
How do you measure campaign performance?
We track verified business metrics including Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPL), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Lead-to-Opportunity conversion rates, and revenue pipeline velocity, accessible through transparent live dashboards.
Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?
Yes. While our headquarters is in Padur, Chennai, we actively serve clients across India, the UAE, Singapore, the US, and the UK with cloud-connected collaboration and regular video reviews.
How can I request a consultation?
