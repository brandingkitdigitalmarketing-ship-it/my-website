COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about partnering with Brandingkit for digital marketing, technology, and business automation.

What digital marketing services does Brandingkit provide?

Brandingkit provides an end-to-end digital growth suite comprising Business Automation (CRM, AI, WhatsApp workflows), Lead Generation Funnels, Search Engine Optimization (SEO & GEO), Website Development, Meta Ads, Google Ads, Social Media Marketing, and Creative Design & Video Editing.

Can Brandingkit manage all our marketing activities?

Yes. We act as your complete outsourced digital growth department. We handle high-level growth strategy, ad creative production, media buying on Google and Meta, landing page design, CRM pipeline setup, automated follow-up sequences, and weekly performance reviews so your leadership team can focus on core operations.

How does your lead generation process work?

Our lead generation framework focuses on intent, not vanity metrics. We deploy hyper-targeted ads on Meta and Google driving traffic to high-converting landing pages. Once an inquiry is captured, our automated system triggers an instant WhatsApp message to the prospect and dispatches the lead into your CRM with sales rep notifications within seconds.

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

Google Ads is a pay-per-click channel that places your business at the top of search results immediately, providing instant qualified traffic for active buyers. SEO (Search Engine Optimization) is a medium-to-long-term asset that builds organic authority, ranking your website continuously without paying for each click. We usually recommend a blended approach for search market dominance.

Can you integrate CRM with WhatsApp Business?

Yes. We specialize in official WhatsApp Business Platform (API) integrations. We connect WhatsApp with popular CRMs like Zoho, LeadSquared, HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and Google Sheets to enable automatic brochure delivery, appointment bookings, catalog sharing, and two-way conversational tracking.

Do you develop websites for small businesses?

Yes. We design and build modern, fast, mobile-first websites tailored for startups, clinics, local service businesses, and expanding enterprises. Our websites are built with conversion-focused UX, clean semantic code, and technical SEO foundations so you rank and convert from day one.

Can I choose individual services or a complete package?

You have complete flexibility. You can engage Brandingkit for standalone requirements — such as a custom website build, an SEO campaign, or Meta Ads management — or select our integrated Digital Growth & Automation Stack for connected end-to-end execution.

How do you measure campaign performance?

We report on real commercial metrics: Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPL), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), Lead-to-Sale Conversion Rate, and total pipeline revenue generated. You receive clear weekly summaries and live dashboard access.

Do you work with businesses outside Chennai?

Yes! While our headquarters is located in Padur, Chennai, we actively work with businesses across Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, Hyderabad, as well as international clients in Dubai, Singapore, and the United States via seamless video consultations and cloud collaboration tools.
